Xevyte Connect Sample Leave Policy

1. Casual Leave (CL)
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per year, accrued at 1 day per month.
- CL cannot be carried forward to the next year.
- Maximum 3 days of CL can be taken consecutively.

2. Sick Leave (SL)
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of Sick Leave per year.
- SL can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
- A medical certificate is required for SL exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3. Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave (PL)
- Employees accrue 1.5 days of EL per month (18 days per year).
- EL requires prior approval of at least 7 days from the reporting manager.
- EL can be carried forward up to a maximum of 45 days.
- EL encashment is allowed at the time of resignation or annually above a balance of 30 days.

4. Maternity Leave
- Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.

5. Paternity Leave
- Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 2 months of the child's birth.

Working Hours:
- Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.
- Grace period of 15 minutes is allowed. Any check-in after 9:45 AM will be marked as late.
- 3 late marks in a month will result in a half-day deduction from Casual Leave.
